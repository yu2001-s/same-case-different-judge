Same Case, Different Judge

Do judgments in Taiwan follow the law and the facts, or do they also depend on who the judge is and the circumstances of the day? This report maps what is known, tests the question on {{n_six_fmt}} drunk-driving judgments from six district courts and {{n_nat_fmt}} cases from all {{nat_courts}} district courts in 2025, and lays out a full research programme.
